## Fan switch: L298N module (L298, Multiwatt15)

Load: 12 V fan, 0.1 A

### 1. Voltage across the fan

    V_fan = Vs - V_drop = 12 V - 1.4 V = 10.6 V

The fan runs at about 88 % of its nominal voltage.

### 2. Power dissipation

Output stage:

    P_out = V_drop * I_L = 1.4 V * 0.1 A = 0.14 W

Quiescent power (upper estimate):

    P_Vs  = Vs * Is  = 12 V * 36 mA = 0.43 W
    P_Vss = Vss * Iss = 5 V * 13 mA = 0.065 W

Total:

    P_tot = 0.14 + 0.43 + 0.065 = 0.635 W

 the 5 V on the module (Vss) is produced by the on-board 78M05 from Vs when the 5V-EN jumper is fitted. Part of this loss is dissipated in the regulator, not in the L298.

### 3. Thermal calculation

R_thJA with a heatsink :

    R_thJA = R_thJC + R_th(case-sink) + R_th(sink-ambient)
           = 3 + 1 + 20 = 24 °C/W   (estimate for the small L298N board heatsink. The heatsink value is not given by the manufacturer.) 

Maximum power:

    P_max = (T_j(max) - T_a) / R_thJA
    P_max = (130 - 25) / 24 = 4.4 W

    T_j = T_a + P_tot * R_thJA
    25 + 0.635 * 24 = 40.24 °C
