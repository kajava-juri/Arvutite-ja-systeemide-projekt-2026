### Fan switch: BC337-25

Load: 12 V fan, 0.1 A

### 1. Base resistor

Minimum base current for saturation:

$$
I_{b(min)} = \frac{I_c}{h_{FE}} = \frac{100\,\text{mA}}{160} = 0.63\,\text{mA}
$$

Design for a safety factor of about 4x, so $I_b$ is about 2.5 mA:

$$
R_b = \frac{V_{GPIO} - V_{BE}}{I_b} = \frac{3.3\,\text{V} - 0.8\,\text{V}}{2.5\,\text{mA}} = 1.0\,\text{k}\Omega
$$

Saturation check (forced gain must be well below $h_{FE}$):

$$
\beta_{forced} = \frac{I_c}{I_b} = \frac{100\,\text{mA}}{2.5\,\text{mA}} = 40 < 160
$$

### 2. Power dissipation

Base resistor:

$$
P_{Rb} = I_b^2 \cdot R_b = (2.5\,\text{mA})^2 \cdot 1\,\text{k}\Omega = 6.25\,\text{mW}
$$

Collector-emitter loss:

$$
P_{CE} = V_{CE(sat)} \cdot I_c = 0.1\,\text{V} \cdot 0.1\,\text{A} = 10\,\text{mW}
$$

Base-emitter loss:

$$
P_{BE} = V_{BE} \cdot I_b = 0.8\,\text{V} \cdot 2.5\,\text{mA} = 2\,\text{mW}
$$

Total transistor dissipation:

$$
P_d = P_{CE} + P_{BE} = 10\,\text{mW} + 2\,\text{mW} = 12\,\text{mW}
$$

Fan supply voltage:

$$
V_{fan} = 12\,\text{V} - V_{CE(sat)} = 11.9\,\text{V}
$$

### 3. Thermal calculation

$$
\begin{aligned}
T_j &= T_a + P_d \cdot R_{thJA} \\
    &= 25\,^\circ\text{C} + 12\,\text{mW} \cdot 200\,\text{K/W} = 27.4\,^\circ\text{C}
\end{aligned}
$$

Maximum allowed power at a given ambient temperature:

$$
\begin{aligned}
P_{max} &= \frac{T_{j(max)} - T_a}{R_{thJA}} \\
P_{max}(25\,^\circ\text{C}) &= \frac{150 - 25}{200} = 625\,\text{mW} \\
P_{max}(60\,^\circ\text{C}) &= \frac{150 - 60}{200} = 450\,\text{mW}
\end{aligned}
$$

The transistor dissipates under 20 mW, which is more than 20x below the limit even at 60 °C ambient, so no heatsink is needed.
