### Fan switch: BC337-25 

Load: 12 V fan, 0.1 A

### 1. Base resistor

Minimum base current for saturation:

    Ib(min) = Ic / hFE = 100 mA / 160 = 0.63 mA

Design for a safety factor of about 4x, so Ib is about 2.5 mA:

    Rb = (V_GPIO - V_BE) / Ib = (3.3 V - 0.8 V) / 2.5 mA = 1.0 kOhm

Saturation check (forced gain must be well below hFE):

    beta_forced = Ic / Ib = 100 mA / 2.5 mA = 40  <  160  

### 2. Power dissipation

Base resistor:

    P_Rb = Ib^2 * Rb = (2.5 mA)^2 * 1 kOhm = 6.25 mW  

Collector-emitter loss:

    P_CE = V_CE(sat) * Ic = 0.1 V * 0.1 A = 10 mW

Base-emitter loss:

    P_BE = V_BE * Ib = 0.8 V * 2.5 mA = 2 mW   
 
Total transistor dissipation:

    P_d = P_CE + P_BE = 10 + 2 = 12 mW   

Fan supply voltage:

    V_fan = 12 V - V_CE(sat) = 11.9 V 

### 3. Thermal calculation

    T_j = T_a + P_d * R_thJA
    25 °C + 12 mW * 200 = 27.4 °C  

Maximum allowed power at a given ambient temperature:

    P_max = (T_j(max) - T_a) / R_thJA
    P_max(25 °C) = (150 - 25) / 200 = 625 mW
    P_max(60 °C) = (150 - 60) / 200 = 450 mW

the transistor dissipates under 20 mW, which is more than 20x below the limit even at 60 °C ambient so no heatsink is needed.
